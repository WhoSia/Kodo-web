# Website Prototype 0.11.5 — Spatial Rhythm, Instrument Depth & Editorial Surface Court

Status: **PASS — PERCEPTION-FIRST / EDITORIAL TIMELINE / PASTEL SIGNAL PALETTE**

## Court decision

This release keeps the split Hero and perception-first identity, then changes the page rhythm rather than adding more public claims.

### RETAIN
- Dark-first theme and warm paper-like light theme.
- Existing typography choices; no font expansion in this round.
- Small NPLUS/Kodo brand marks in the navigation.
- Perception-first research narrative.
- White constellation / graph language as the dominant ambient motif.

### MUTATE
- Perception: four equal cards → asymmetric 2×2 editorial lab surface.
- Hero instrument: abstract graph → restrained labels, ticks and pastel signal nodes.
- Roadmap: five narrow cards → one continuous editorial timeline rail.
- Cards: stronger dark-mode outlines and clearer hierarchy.
- Palette: grayscale-only surface → dark neutral base with pastel cyan, mint, lavender, blue and peach signals.
- Footer: paired NPLUS and Kodo marks at comparable visual weight.

### RETIRE
- Public GitHub CTA.
- Large Hero Kodo logo billboard.
- Five skinny Roadmap cards.
- Equal visual weight for every Perception item.

## Research boundary

The page foregrounds screen-to-state perception because that is the current engineering bottleneck. It does not claim that screen-based game AI is unprecedented. Multi-rate perception remains an engineering hypothesis until baseline measurements establish useful cadence and performance trade-offs.
