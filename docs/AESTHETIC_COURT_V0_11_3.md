## One-person RAVEL aesthetic court

### Art Director
The large Hero logo was visually redundant. It competed with the headline and made the right side read as a logo billboard rather than a research instrument. **RETIRE large Hero logo.**

### Composition Director
The Hero copy sat too low because the large right-hand panel controlled the vertical center. Shrinking the right visual to a square instrument field and lifting the copy improves first-screen balance. **MUTATE vertical composition.**

### Brand Director
The Kodo crest works better as a small persistent identifier beside the Kodo wordmark. A dedicated cropped crest asset is used to avoid shrinking the full logo into illegibility. **RETAIN small crest only.**

### Interface Director
Perception, Roadmap and Progress retain cards, but dark-mode outlines are strengthened. The System section is opened into a schematic rail to reduce repeated-dashboard rhythm. **RECOMBINE cards + open schematic.**

### Motion / Atmosphere Director
Ambient graph fields remain secondary. The Hero receives the dominant constellation cluster; section-level graph fields stay quieter. **C + D composition.**

### Skeptical Reviewer
Avoid generic neon AI visual language. No glow walls, glassmorphism, or decorative gradients beyond restrained instrument-field lighting. The graph is decorative and does not pretend to encode measured data.
