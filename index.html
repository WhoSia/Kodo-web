01 / PERCEPTION

Pixels first. Policy second.

Before an agent can learn, Kodo has to recover the game from the screen: who is where, what is ready, and what the map looks like.

YOLO / ULTRALYTICS

Entities + walls

Detect players, allies, enemies and map geometry.

OPENCV

STATE RECOVERY

Position + readiness

Recover wall and player positions plus super, gadget and hypercharge signals.

HARDEST SIGNAL
